Our Story

From Philly Roots to Birmingham's Best

In March 2002, Anthony "T-Bone" Crawford opened his first restaurant in Center Point, Alabama with one mission: bring authentic Philadelphia cheesesteak culture to the South. No shortcuts. No imitations. The real thing.

Over two decades later, T-Bone's is Birmingham's most-loved cheesesteak destination — a testament to real ingredients, relentless craft, and a community that keeps coming back.
